A few useful answers

Questions, considered.

Who can request a bulk quotation?

Corporate offices, schools, colleges, small businesses, retailers and other organizations can submit an enquiry.

Can I attach my existing supply list?

Yes. Use the file upload field to attach a supported document or image. The live form integration should define the final file size and type limits.

Are the prices shown on the website bulk prices?

Displayed product prices are sample catalogue prices. Bulk pricing is confirmed separately after reviewing your product list and quantities.

Does submitting the form place an order?

No. It sends an enquiry only once connected to an email or backend service. A quotation and order confirmation would follow separately.
