Before you enquire

A few pricing questions.

Are these subscription plans?

No. Penora is a product catalogue and enquiry-based retail concept. These are supply pathways, not recurring software or membership plans.

How is bulk pricing determined?

It depends on requested products, quantities, availability, delivery location and timing. Share your list for a tailored quotation.

Can I request a mix of categories?

Yes. Use the bulk enquiry form to describe multiple product categories and attach a purchase list if available.
